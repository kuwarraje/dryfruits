Our Premium Sourcing Story

Sourced With Purity

The Essence of W-Biz Dry Fruits

Established with a singular focus on luxury gourmet quality, W-Biz Dry Fruits has grown into the premier boutique dry fruits shop in Wakad, Pune. Our boutique was born to answer one critical need: access to authentic, farm-fresh, premium-grade nuts and dried delicacies in Pimpri-Chinchwad and the Mumbai-Pune Expressway area.

We work in perfect coordination with high-grade, sustainable international growers. From the lush valleys of California that produce our premium almonds, to the sun-bathed mountains of Turkey yielding succulent figs, and the Middle Eastern orchards delivering dark Medjool dates – every single batch represents excellence.

We firmly believe that health shopping should be a luxurious experience. That is why our boutique is curated with custom wood shelves, ambient gold-hued lighting, and vacuum-sealed packaging layers that preserve raw natural oils, vital nutrients, and perfect crispness.

Uncompromising Hygiene

Our Multi-Step Quality Assurance

Your health is our utmost priority. Learn how we handle our products to guarantee fresh and hygienic dry fruits.

Triple Sorting Protocol

Every single almond, cashew, and fig undergoes absolute visual, scale, and sensory sorting. We remove broken shells, micro-dust, and odd sizes, leaving only giant, flawless dry fruits.

Moisture Controlled Pack

We seal our dry fruits using nitrogen-flushed vacuum technology. This controls oxygen decay, entirely stops mold formation, and locks in natural organic moisture ratios for crunchiness.

Organic Certifications

We prioritize zero-pesticide, organic farming. Our mixed nuts and dry fruit gift boxes contain sun-dried, chemical-free delicacies directly compliant with top Indian food safety guidelines.
